# Prose & Writing Clichés

## Pre-Draft / Review Protocol

Run these checks before submitting any written output:

1. **Structure**: Vary sentence rhythm. No mechanical "X, then Y" triple-beat lists. No staccato fragments stacked together. Start with presence, not atmosphere. End on action, decision, or consequence.
2. **Language**: Zero adverbs on dialogue tags. Never use "something" as an emotion placeholder. Don't describe silence or atmosphere as an active force. Name specific emotions.
3. **Physical Description**: Give characters distinct tells. Never default to jaw/throat/breath/eye clichés.
4. **Metaphor**: No gravitational language for attraction. No temperature as emotion. No dead metaphors.

---

## Banned Words & Phrases

### Physical Tells (DO NOT USE)
- jaw tightens / jaw clenches
- throat bobs / throat tightens
- breath catches / holds their breath (as emotional signal)
- eyes darken / eyes soften / eyes search his/hers
- hands curl into fists
- spine stiffens
- shoulders tense

### Vagueness / Filler
- a wave of [emotion] crashes over him/her
- feels it in his bones
- the room feels smaller
- something shifts between them
- the air changes
- hangs in the air

### Hollow Descriptors
- raw (as in "raw emotion", "raw need")
- visceral
- primal
- bone-deep
- paper-thin
- electric (as in "electric tension")
- charged (as atmosphere descriptor)

### Dialogue Adverbs (use zero)
- softly, quietly, carefully, flatly, evenly, roughly, gently, firmly, tightly

### Cinematic Filler
- for a long moment
- time stretched / time slowed
- light spills / light pools
- neon glow / amber light / golden hour (as mood-setting opener)
- the silence stretched

### AI Vocabulary (flagged, never use)
- tapestry, landscape, delve, foster, underscore, showcase
- pivotal, crucial, profound, palpable
- it's worth noting, it's important to note
- in today's world, in the modern landscape
- navigate (as metaphor for dealing with something)
- embark (on a journey)
- at the end of the day
- game-changer, paradigm shift, synergy

---

## The Corrective Framework

| Banned Element | Ask Instead |
|----------------|-------------|
| Physical tell (jaw tightens) | How does *this specific character* show this emotion — what's their personal habit? |
| Vague interiority (something shifts) | What does an observer *literally see*? What does the character *specifically think*? |
| Hollow descriptor (raw, visceral) | What is the concrete sensation or action that earns this word? Name it. |
| Dialogue adverb | Can the line carry its own tone without adverb support? If not, rewrite the line. |
| Cinematic filler (for a long moment) | Cut it. The beat is implied by what comes before and after. |
| AI vocabulary (delve, tapestry) | Replace with the precise, plain-language version of what you mean. |

## Good vs. Bad Examples

**Bad**: *Her breath caught as his eyes darkened. For a long moment, the air between them felt charged.*

**Good**: *She looked at the door, not at him. The wrong thing to say would be anything.*

---

**Bad**: *In today's fast-paced landscape, it's crucial to delve into the pivotal aspects of this profound topic.*

**Good**: *This matters because it changes what you do next.*

---

**Bad**: *"I don't care," he said flatly.*

**Good**: *"I don't care." He turned the mug in his hands.*
